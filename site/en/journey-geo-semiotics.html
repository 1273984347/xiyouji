GEO-SEMIOTICS OF THE PILGRIMAGE

Localization · Delocalization · Relocalization — Spatial Flow under a Triple Mechanism

STEP 01 · LOCALIZATION

Localization

Mount Huaguo Water-Curtain Cave · Ch.1
The Stone Monkey is born on Mount Huaguo, where its native terrain and ecology ground "self-nature" — the monkey completes its naming and crowning in this blessed cave-heaven; geography is identity. This is a primordial "localization": nature bestows meaning.
ref: Ch.1 line 522-523
STEP 02 · DE-LOCALIZATION

Delocalization

The pilgrimage route itself · from Ch.13
When Xuanzang leaves Chang'an, the 108,000-li westward road suspends "place" — passing through Chechi, Zhuizi, Miefa and other kingdoms, geographic difference is compressed into a semiotic testing ground of ideology, body-politics, and sovereign violence.
ref: Ch.13 line 1343-1346
STEP 03 · RE-LOCALIZATION

Relocalization

Return to Spirit Mountain–Eastern Land · Ch.98
After reaching Spirit Mountain and obtaining the scriptures, "Eastern Land" becomes the destination of return — meaning lands anew. This is not a return to the origin, but a re-encoding of what the journey gained into the homeland, forming a spiral closure of the sign.
ref: Ch.98 line 7001

Key Insights

The 7 nodes and 7 relations are not a route map but a three-step mechanism: one place of origin (Mount Huaguo Water-Curtain Cave, Ch. 1), one capital of departure (Chang’an, Ch. 13) and one destination (the Spirit Mountain, Ch. 8 and Ch. 98), with three transit kingdoms in between — Chechi Kingdom (Ch. 44, ideological), Zhuizi Kingdom (Ch. 68, body politics) and Miefa Kingdom (Ch. 84, sovereign violence). The chapter numbers show when the mechanism works: all three transit kingdoms fall at or after Ch. 44, i.e. in the second half of the book, so place only dissolves once the ordeal structure is already in place. The last two edges close the loop rather than open a new one — the Spirit Mountain hands the pilgrim back to the same Chang’an he left — which is why the model calls the last stage re-localization rather than a second journey.
