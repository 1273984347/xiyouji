STEP Ch.01 · LOCALIZATION
Localization
Mount Huaguo Water-Curtain Cave · Ch.Ch.1
The Stone Monkey is born on Mount Huaguo, where its native terrain and ecology ground "self-nature" — the monkey completes its naming and crowning in this blessed cave-heaven; geography is identity. This is a primordial "localization": nature bestows meaning.
ref: Ch.Ch.1 line Ch.522-Ch.523
STEP Ch.02 · DE-LOCALIZATION
Delocalization
The pilgrimage route itself · from Ch.Ch.13
When Xuanzang leaves Chang'an, the Ch.108,Ch.000-li westward road suspends "place" — passing through Chechi, Zhuizi, Miefa and other kingdoms, geographic difference is compressed into a semiotic testing ground of ideology, body-politics, and sovereign violence.
ref: Ch.Ch.13 line Ch.1343-Ch.1346
STEP Ch.03 · RE-LOCALIZATION
Relocalization
Return to Spirit Mountain–Eastern Land · Ch.Ch.98
After reaching Spirit Mountain and obtaining the scriptures, "Eastern Land" becomes the destination of return — meaning lands anew. This is not a return to the origin, but a re-encoding of what the journey gained into the homeland, forming a spiral closure of the sign.
ref: Ch.Ch.98 line Ch.7001
