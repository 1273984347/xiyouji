③ Spacetime Trajectory Insights

[Origin] Chapters 1–12 form the "prequel space". Events cluster in three places — Chang'an / Two Frontiers Mountain / Eagle Sorrow Gorge — yet span 12% of the timeline. This is the spatial groundwork for Wukong's growth (Havoc in Heaven) + Sanzang's origins + the genesis of the pilgrimage. Geography stands still while time flows.
[Leap] Chapter 13 is the "spatial leap point". Sanzang exits the west gate of Chang'an and crosses Two Frontiers Mountain in the first geographic leap. Afterward, each new location corresponds to 2–4 chapters, forming a stable "location–chapter" coupling rhythm.
[Dense] The middle stretch, chapters 26–75, is the "dense event band". Within 50 chapters it gathers 32 key events, covering ten strongholds — White Tiger Ridge / Flat-Top Mountain / Fire Cloud Cave / River of Heaven / Golden Helmet Mountain / Womanland / Flaming Mountain / Lesser Thunderclap / Spider Silk Cave / Lion-Camel Ridge. This is the "main battlefield" of the pilgrimage narrative.
[Sparse] Chapters 91–100 are the "sparse event band". Only 5 events occur within 10 chapters, concentrated from the Kingdom of India to Lingshan. The narrative pace visibly slows, returning to the theme of "attaining Buddhahood" — once at Lingshan, geography yields to the mind.
[Turnback] Chapters 27–31 are the "spatial turnback band". After thrice beating the White-Bone Demon, Wukong is banished; Sanzang retreats to Baoxiang Kingdom, meets the Yellow-Robed Demon, and is forced to summon Wukong back. This is the only "geographic regression" passage in the whole novel, symbolizing that "once the mind-monkey is lost, all prior merit is forfeit."
[Peak] Lion-Camel Ridge (chapters 74–77) is the "spacetime peak". Four chapters concentrate on a single location, carrying three consecutive battle events. Geography holds still while time stretches — the highest "local density" passage in the entire book.
