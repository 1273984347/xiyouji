    Definition: Artifacts in Xiyouji exist in mutual generation and counter-relations, forming a weapon ecology rather than a single dominant item. All metal artifacts are countered by the Vajra Ring, and the Vajra Ring is countered by the Palm-Leaf Fan, forming a counter-chain. The three golden cuffs cannot be broken by external force and are removed only “upon attaining Buddhahood”. The Jade Vase is invinciblebut is used only to subdue Red Boy — a typical project-phase artifact.
