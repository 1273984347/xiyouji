I · Heavenly Court Sentencing Economics

SENTENCING ECONOMICS · An Atlas of Imbalance in Status-Based Justice
Sentencing economics: the severity of punishment does not depend entirely on the severity of the crime, but is dually modulated by identity/background (inside or outside the establishment) and political need (whether stability is affected). Kui Wood Wolf (seizing a princess + cannibalism) is merely punished with tending the furnace, while the Jing River Dragon King (reducing the rainfall) is beheaded — same crime, different punishment: sentencing is severely imbalanced.
