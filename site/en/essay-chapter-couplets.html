The Hundred Couplets

ESSAY E9 · SUMMARY TRANSLATION
Every chapter wears a seven-character couplet like a frontispiece — part prosody, part plot-summary, part Eight-Legged Essay.
A summary translation of docs/05-诗词歌赋/回目对联分析专题.md. The Chinese original analyzes all 100 chapter title-couplets through three coordinates — prosody, chapter-poetics, and the structural homology with Ming Eight-Legged Essays.

Three Coordinates ↔ how to read a title

  • Prosody (Wang Li): level/oblique tone parallelism, part-of-speech matching, equal length.
  • Chapter-poetics (Qi Gong, Liu Shide): the title as the chapter's "frontispiece" and abstract.
  • Eight-Legged homology (Qian Zhongshu): structural kinship with the Ming exam essay's paired "breaking-open" lines.

Prosody ↔ 7 + 7 characters

All 100 chapter titles are seven-character couplets (fourteen characters total). Tone patterns follow the rule "flexible on 1-3-5, strict on 2-4-6." Chapter 1 — "Lingu-gen yu-yun yuan-liu chu / Xin-xing xiu-chi da-dao sheng" — pairs level and oblique on the even positions exactly as Wang Li prescribes. The regularity actually exceeds that of Investiture of the Gods or Jin Ping Mei, evidence of Wu Cheng'en's deliberate craft.

Five Types ↔ semantic relations

  • Causal — Ch.1: "the spirit-root issues forth / mind-nature cultivates the Great Way."
  • Action — Ch.14: "the mind-monkey returns to the right / the six thieves vanish."
  • Scene — Ch.22: "Bajie battles the Quicksand River / Mokṣa receives Wujing."
  • Symbolic — Ch.58: "the two minds disturb the great cosmos / the one body hardly cultivates true nirvāṇa."
  • Power-structure — Ch.8: "the Buddha makes scriptures for the West / Guanyin bears the edict to Chang'an."

The occasional looseness is intentional — some couplets sacrifice meter for imagery, raising their philosophical charge.

Narrative Function ↔ 14 characters, one chapter

A title-couplet is a fourteen-character chapter abstract. Chapter 22's "Bajie battles / Mokṣa receives" compresses an entire 起承转合 arc — conflict in the upper line, resolution in the lower. The couplets therefore double as a built-in table of contents and a forecast of each chapter's emotional key.
