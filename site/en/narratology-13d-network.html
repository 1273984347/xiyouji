I · Thirteen-Dimension Narratology Framework Overview

OVERVIEW: 13-DIMENSION NARRATOLOGY FRAMEWORK
Definition:The thirteen-dimension narratology network integrates the W161–W174 twelve-dimension narratology topics with the four new W182–W185 dimensions (Affective Narratology, Historical Narratology, Meta-Narratology, Unreliable Narration), dividing the narratological study of Journey to the West into sixteen interrelated dimensions grouped under seven categories: Seven-Sense Narratology (Time/Space/Auditory/Olfactory/Tactile/Visual/Gustatory), Textual Narratology (Intertext/Rhetoric/Cognitive), Gender Narratology, Cultural Narratology, Affective Narratology, Historical Narratology, and Meta-Narratology (Metanarrative/Unreliable). It builds on the W168 Six-Senses Narratology and the W175 Twelve-Dimension Narratology to realize the theoretical expansion "seven senses → twelve dimensions → thirteen dimensions", constructing a complete narratological analysis network covering space-time, the senses, intertextuality, rhetoric, cognition, gender, the postcolonial, affect, history, and metanarrative.

II · Thirteen-Dimension Narratology Force-Directed Graph

FORCE-DIRECTED GRAPH: 16 DIMENSIONS & 28 INTER-DIMENSION EDGES
Definition:The force-directed graph takes the sixteen narratology dimensions as nodes; node color distinguishes the seven categories (seven-sense steel blue / textual olive green / gender twilight purple / cultural ochre orange / affective vermilion / historical deep chestnut / metanarrative deep indigo), and node size reflects academic value. Edges between dimensions are labeled with relation type (Spatio-Sensory / Theoretical-Textual / Cross-Domain), and edge thickness corresponds to connection strength. It reveals the internal structure of thirteen-dimension narratology — the seven-sense dimensions cluster tightly, the textual-theoretical dimensions connect laterally, and the four new W182–W185 dimensions form a vertical chain "Affective → Historical → Metanarrative → Unreliable".

III · Dimension → Theorist → Application Three-Layer Sankey Diagram

SANKEY: DIMENSIONS → THEORISTS → APPLICATIONS
Definition:The Sankey diagram shows a three-layer flow: sixteen dimensions → related theorists → Journey to the West application cases. Each dimension connects to its representative theorist (e.g., "Time" to Genette, "Space" to Bakhtin, "Intertext" to Kristeva, "Affective" to Faber, "Historical" to Hayden White, "Metanarrative" to Patricia Waugh), and theorists flow onward to concrete Journey to the West application cases. Flow thickness corresponds to a theory’s application weight in Journey to the West studies, revealing the path of conversion from abstract theory to textual analysis.

IV · Thirteen-Dimension Narratology Radar Chart (4 Metrics)

RADAR: 16 DIMENSIONS × 4 METRICS
Definition:The radar chart evaluates each narratology dimension on four metrics: theoretical depth (maturity of the theoretical system), JTTW application (coverage of Journey to the West textual analysis), term count (size of the core terminology), and academic impact (research contribution and influence). The larger the radar area, the stronger the dimension’s overall standing in the thirteen-dimension narratology framework. Intertextuality (Intertext), Temporal Narratology (Time), and Unreliable Narration perform most strongly overall, while the tactile and olfactory dimensions are emerging growth points.
