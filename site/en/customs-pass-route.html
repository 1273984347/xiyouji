③ Courier-Route Insights

[Issuance] Chapter 12 · Imperial Document. After the Great Water and Land Assembly at Huasheng Temple, Emperor Taizong personally bestows the passport (Ch.12 line 51)—the document is the materialization of the "imperial title": thereafter, at every kingdom the master and disciples "present the pass, have it inspected, and are released through", folding the scripture-seeking journey into that kingdom's sovereignty (W370 courier–document dimension).
[Verified] Nine Kingdoms Exchanged · Imperial Network. Baoxiang (29) / Wuji (37) / Chechi (45) / Womanland (54) / Jisai (62) / Zhuzi (68) / Bhikṣu (78) / Yuhua (88) / Tianzhu (93)—nine places verified. Exchanging the pass is the ritual of "crossing borders", and proof of the spatial order of "coexisting kingdoms" in the JTTW world.
[Fallen Kingdom] Lion-Camel Kingdom · Nowhere to Verify the Pass. Chapter 74, line 41: Lion-Camel Kingdom's city is seized by the Roc, its king and officials slaughtered—the imperial document loses force within the demon-king's domain; "no kingdom left to verify" is the most radical suspension of order on the journey.
[Altered] Miefa Kingdom · Identity Altered. Chapter 84, lines 3/17: under the monk-killing edict, the master and disciples shave their heads, change their names, and slip out of the city—the pass unverified, identity altered first: the imperial document's authority yields to survival strategy.
[Transmission] Lingshan · Scriptures Granted Without Verification. Chapter 98: scriptures are granted (line 1) yet turn out wordless (line 43), as Ānanda and Kāśyapa demand a gratuity—scripture and passport part ways at Lingshan: the passport governs "people crossing borders", the true scriptures govern "the Dharma crossing borders"; in Chapter 100 (line 7) the pass is re-verified and the five saints attain fruition (line 45), closing the passport's mission.
