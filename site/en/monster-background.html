Monster Background Theory

Mounts Descend to Earth · The Fate of Demons with Backing

This page is a focused deep-dive into "Monster Sociology", zeroing in on the book's sharpest satire — whether a demon lives or dies depends not on its strength but on its backing. The data draws on the 100 chapters of the original Journey to the West, covering 69 demons in total. A note on methodology: "background-type survival rate" aggregates by each demon's backing type, while "disposition count" aggregates by final outcome; the two measures have a cross error of ±2 (stemming from differing judgments about which backing category escaped demons belong to), which does not affect the core conclusion.
