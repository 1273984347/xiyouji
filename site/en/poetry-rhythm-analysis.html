① Cipai Distribution Treemap

Across the whole of Journey to the West, the poems fall into 7 categories: 3 cipai (Xijiangyue / Linjiangxian / Mantingfang) + opening poems + chapter couplets (100 chapters) + character fu + scenery poems. Chapter couplets are the most numerous (100), forming the skeleton of the book’s poetry; the cipai—only 3 patterns, 3 poems—are the core sample for prosodic analysis. The treemap uses area for share: the smaller a block, the fewer poems in that category (e.g., each cipai has just 1 poem); hover for category details, and the legend lists all 7 categories with their shares.

② Ping-ze Rhyme-Tone Distribution Heatmap

A matrix of the 7 A6 poetry samples × the level/oblique binary of rhyme tones. Color depth shows the percentage of level-tone or oblique-tone characters in each poem. "Mantingfang" has the highest level-tone share (72%, the most serene), "chapter couplets" are the most balanced (50/50, neatly antithetical), and "opening poems" lean oblique (40%, strongly rhythmic).

④ Five Poetry Layers Stacked Chart

Stack the poems in the 10 chapter ranges by 5 layers: opening poem (kaipian) / chapter couplet (huimu) / character fu (renwu) / scenery poem (jingwu) / cipai. Chapter couplets are a constant 10 per segment (one per chapter), forming a stable base; scenery poems and character fu fluctuate through the middle, reflecting narrative density; cipai appear only in the first and last segments (Xijiangyue at the opening, Mantingfang at the close).

⑤ Prosodic Feature Distribution Box Plot

Statistical distribution of the "level-tone share" across the 7 A6 poetry samples (min/Q1/median/Q3/max). Box height reflects dispersion, and the middle line is the median. The cipai group (Xijiangyue / Linjiangxian / Mantingfang) has the highest median level-tone share and the smallest dispersion (most regular prosody); chapter couplets are the most tightly clustered (neat antithesis); opening poems are the most dispersed (strong narrative causes fluctuation).

Prosodic Feature Highlights

[Cipai] All three cipai have level-tone share ≥ 68%. Mantingfang leads at 72%, Linjiangxian follows at 68%, and Xijiangyue at 70%. Cipai emphasize level/oblique alternation, and level-tone dominance reads as serene and elegant.
[Couplet] Chapter couplets hold a perfect 50/50 level/oblique balance. All 100 couplets are strictly antithetical with opposed tones—the book’s "standard reference frame" for prosody.
[Opening] The opening poem is 60% level / 40% oblique. The oblique tilt creates a solemn narrative tone, matching the epic opening keynote of "before chaos parted, heaven and earth were troubled."
