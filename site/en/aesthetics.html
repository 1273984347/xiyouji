I · Character Fashion Evolution

The fashion_level (1-10) of the five pilgrim protagonists shifts across life stages. The horizontal axis is the stage index and the vertical axis the fashion level — note Wukong’s “U-curve”: Stone Monkey (1) → Great Sage Equal to Heaven (9) → Tiger-Skin Kilt (4) → Victorious Fighting Buddha (10).

II · The Twelve-Color Spectrum

Grouped by category: Buddha Gold + royal Vermilion = the solemn base palette; Ink Black spans Heaven and the demonic; Jade Cyan and Dark Green denote the Daoist arts and the wilderness; Bone White and Tiger-Skin Yellow alone mark horror and the Pilgrim.

III · Scene Aesthetic Rating Heatmap

14 key scenes sorted by level (1-10), with color depth corresponding to the rating. Heaven and the Buddha lands sit at level 10, demon lairs at level 1-2 — a striking aesthetic polarization.

IV · Style School Proportions

Seven style schools proportioned by scenes_count. Mundane · Plain leads with 12 scenes, Horror · Demonic follows with 8, and Solemn · Supreme has 5 — worldly bustle and demonic dread together outweigh the solemnity of gods and Buddhas.

V · Character Stage Cards

10 characters × their life stages. Each card’s left vertical bar uses its primary color, and the number on the right is its fashion_level. From heavenly generals to demons to Buddha countenances, the sartorial cultivation arc of the five pilgrims is clear at a glance.
