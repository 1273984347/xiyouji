① Belbin Nine-Role Coverage Radar

The pilgrim team's 5 members cover 8 of the 9 Belbin roles, missing only the "Monitor Evaluator" — precisely the root cause of the team repeatedly falling into the "Wukong decides, Tang Sanzang vetoes" conflict. No one calmly evaluates options, causing decision-making to oscillate.

④ Group Dynamics: Goal-Alignment Analysis

The five members' surface goals align (the pilgrimage), but their deep motivations diverge. Only Tang Sanzang has high goal alignment; Bajie's is lowest. This "surface consensus / deep divergence" structure is the root cause of group-dynamics conflict. Every time Bajie proposes splitting the luggage, a deep motivation (returning to lay life) rises to the surface.

Key finding: The decision triangle (Tang Sanzang → Wukong → Bajie) overlaps with the conflict triangle (Tang Sanzang vs. Wukong → Bajie echoes) — Wukong is both the executor and the conflict party. This role overlap means every execution by Wukong may trigger conflict.

Power structure: The separation of external legitimacy (Guanyin / Tathāgata's commission → Tang Sanzang) from internal legitimacy (greatest ability → Wukong) is the power-structure root cause of the "White-Bone Demon" conflict.
