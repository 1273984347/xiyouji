A Hundred Chapters, Four Movements

The hundred-chapter novel is not a flat sequence but a structured arc. The project's per-chapter metadata — the couplet title, principal figures, and localities for every chapter — lets us read the whole as four movements: a rebel's origin, a call to mission, the long road, and a return transfigured. The tables below are the project's own extracted metadata (zero invented facts); each couplet is the chapter's original two-line title.
