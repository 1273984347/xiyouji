I · Six-Senses Narratology Overview

SIX-SENSES NARRATOLOGY OVERVIEW
Definition:Six-senses narratology uses seven sensory dimensions — time, space, hearing, smell, touch, sight, and taste — as coordinates to construct a perception-narrative mapping system for Journey to the West. Each sensory dimension corresponds to one topic (W161-W167), encompassing 28 core terms, 4 theorists, 5 Journey cases, and 4 layers of narrative structure. Together, the seven senses form a four-layer narratological analytical framework of perception–narrative–structure–case.
