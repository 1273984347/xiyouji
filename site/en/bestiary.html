Not Monsters — an Ecology

The novel's demons are not random frights; read as a population they form an ecology with niches, food-chains, and a striking 73% extinction rate. The project models 30 named species across 4 social types and tracks 42 symbiosis links — most of them the "reclamation" of a demon by its heavenly patron. Below, the headline numbers, the four ways a demon organizes itself, and the capability outliers.

Four Ways to Be a Monster

独狼型 LONE WOLF

单兵作战，无家无援，靠智谋或变身术生存，缺乏跨山头救援。

Examples: 白骨精 · 蜘蛛精 · 九头虫

家族型 FAMILY-CLAN

以血缘为纽带，跨山头联防，讲究家产继承与子代培养。

Examples: 牛魔王家族 · 金角银角兄弟

联盟型 ALLIANCE

异姓结盟，按实力排座，共享地盘与情报，但内部脆性高。

Examples: 狮驼岭三魔 · 车迟国三妖

官僚型 CELESTIAL BUREAUCRAT

本身有天上编制，下凡属出差或擅离职守，归宿是归位而非消灭。

Examples: 黄袍怪（奎木狼）· 黄风怪（灵吉坐骑）

Capability Extremes

  • 最强法力: 大鹏金翅雕 — 10/10 (如来亲舅父·佛祖亲率五百罗汉降伏)
  • 最智谋: 六耳猕猴 — 9/10 (假悟空难辨·骗过天庭众神)
  • 最悲剧: 六耳猕猴 — 结局 1/10 (被如来识破即被悟空一棒打死)
  • 最幸运: 红孩儿 — 收编善财童子 (观音收为侍童·得正果)

The deepest pattern: the most dangerous foes are rarely the wild ones. The apex predators — the Golden-Winged Great Peng (magic 10/10, Buddha's own uncle) and the Buffalo King (power 9/10, head of a family empire) — are insiders. Wild demons get killed; connected demons get reclaimed. The bestiary is, quietly, a satire of bureaucracy.
