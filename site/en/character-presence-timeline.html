6 · Key Insights

KEY INSIGHTS
Two density peaks: chapters 24-30 (late team assembly) and 74-82 (late-pilgrimage climax) show twin peaks of character density, reflecting the narrative's pacing control.
Wukong never misses: Sun Wukong appears from chapter 1 to chapter 100 — the only main character present throughout — embodying the core role of the "Mind-Ape."
Tripitaka arrives late: Tripitaka first appears in chapter 11 (line 1219), after the "previous-incarnation" narratives of Wukong, Bajie, and Sha — reflecting the delayed start of the "pilgrimage project."
Short antagonist lifecycles: most antagonists last only 3-5 chapters, reflecting the "one demon per ordeal" narrative structure.
Guanyin stays present: Guanyin appears from chapter 8 onward (present in 98 chapters), an "observer" role threading through the whole book.
Assembly sequence: the pilgrimage team assembles — Wukong (ch.14) + White Dragon Horse (ch.15) + Bajie (ch.19) + Sha (ch.22) — matching the Forming stage of the W121 five-companions network.
