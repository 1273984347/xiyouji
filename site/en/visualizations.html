Visualizations — An English Tour

GUIDE · 85 INTERACTIVE PAGES
A categorized gallery of the project's D3.js visualizations, with what each shows and how to read it.
A curated English index of the ~85 interactive visualizations under site/data/. Each card links to the live page and summarizes its content. Groupings follow the project's own analytical tracks (tribulations, characters, monsters, narrative, poetics, systems, applied readings, meta-tools).

Why a Tour? ↔ 85 pages, eight clusters

The Chinese site ships 85 interactive D3.js pages. Most are pure-Chinese UI, which can daunt a non-Chinese reader. This page is an English gallery and reading guide: every entry links to the live visualization, with a one-line note on what it shows and how to read it. Pages open under site/data/; most need no server (they embed their data, or fetch locally).
