③ Relationship Evolution Insights

[Formation] Stage 1 (Chapters 1–25) is the "team-formation period". The core relationships are 4 master–disciple links (Sanzang → Wukong / Bajie / Sha Wujing / White Dragon Horse) plus the early recruit (Black Bear Spirit). Guanyin acts as a "broker node" linking Chang'an with the pilgrimage band, while Taibai Jinxing and Great Immortal Zhenyuan appear as transitional figures.
[Expansion] Stage 2 (Chapters 26–50) is the "hostility-expansion period". The pilgrimage band begins large-scale contact with the demons: White Bone Demon / Yellow Robe Demon / Gold and Silver Horns / Red Boy / King of Inspiration / Green Ox Spirit. At the same time, "Aid" edges surge — Guanyin / Lingji Bodhisattva / Taishang Laojun / Manjushri step in one after another. This is the densest stage of the network.
[Climax] Stage 3 (Chapters 51–75) is the "relationship-climax period". The Bull Demon King family at the Flaming Mountains (a concentrated burst of Kin edges) + the true and false Monkey King (Tathāgata exposes the Six-Eared Macaque) + the three demons of Lion-Camel Ridge (the Buddha appears in person). Kin edges peak in this stage, and the upper echelons of the Buddhist realm (Tathāgata / Maitreya) begin direct intervention.
[Convergence] Stage 4 (Chapters 76–100) is the "relationship-convergence period". Hostile edges plummet to single digits, and most demons are reclaimed by their masters (White Deer / Rat Demon / Jade Rabbit Demon). The remaining edges concentrate on the "path to Buddhahood" — the "conferring of Buddhahood" relationship between Sanzang's band and Tathāgata / Guanyin. The network converges from "many-to-many" into a binary structure of "pilgrimage band ↔ Buddhist realm".
[Broker] Guanyin is the "network broker node" of the whole book. She is active in all four stages, linking the pilgrimage band, Heaven, the Buddhist realm, and the demons. Her appearance count is second only to Wukong, but her "betweenness" is far higher — nearly all cross-faction rescues are relayed through Guanyin.
[Family] "Kin edges" concentrate in the family demons of Stages 2 and 3. Bull Demon King–Princess Iron Fan–Red Boy (a family of three), Azure Lion–White Elephant–Roc (three sworn demons), Gold Horn–Silver Horn (brothers). Family-based demons are the core of the network's "subgroups" and the most difficult opponents.
