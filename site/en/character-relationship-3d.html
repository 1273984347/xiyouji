Key Insights

What the 22-node relation network actually says

  • 01Sun Wukong alone carries degree 12 — 12 of the book's 32 relations (37.5%) — Tripitaka follows at 7, and Guanyin and the Jade Emperor at 5 each; every other node keeps 4 or fewer ties.
  • 02All four edges between Wukong and the Heavenly Court are conflict edges — 1 Rebel (against the Jade Emperor) and 3 Enemy (Erlang Shen, Li Jing, Nezha): the heavenly court never appears as an ally of the pilgrimage in this network.
  • 03Kin and hierarchy are recorded as carefully as combat — 17 distinct relation types across 32 edges: Enemy × 5, Subordinate × 5, Master-Disciple × 4, Father-Son × 3, Brothers × 2, Colleague × 2, plus one-off types such as Husband-Wife, Mother-Son and Sworn Foe.
  • 04The smallest faction is still tied into the pilgrimage — the demon realm holds 6 nodes, the pilgrimage and heaven 5 each, buddhism 4, and the dragon clan only 2 (the East and West Sea Dragon Kings) — yet the White Dragon Horse's father-son edge to the West Sea Dragon King keeps that 2-node cluster attached to the main journey.
