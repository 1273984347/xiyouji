I · Introduction to the Female Demon Network

INTRODUCTION: MONSTER FEMALE NETWORK
Definition: The Monster Female Network centers on 14 female demons in Journey to the West, joined by three female deity collectors (Bodhisattva Pilanpo, Bodhisattva Guanyin, and Old Mother of Lishan), forming a three-layer network of "seven Spider Sisters — lone demons — female deities". The seven Spider Sisters are the only female-demon group in the novel to appear as a sisterhood (Chapters 72–73, Pansi Cave), forming a sisterly community that is close within and distant without; the other seven female demons are scattered across the whole pilgrimage (Chapters 27–95), either trapped by desire (the Scorpion Demon, Apricot Fairy, and Rat Demon), fighting over a husband (Princess Iron Fan and the Jade-Faced Fox), or disguising themselves by shape-shifting (the White Bone Demon and the Jade Rabbit Demon) — reflecting the Ming novel’s double imagining of female desire and danger. The three female deities, as collectors and subduers, form a mirror image of "orthodox women" set against the demons. This chart uses five views — force-directed, Sankey, radar, timeline, and summary — continuing the W155/W151 power-relations series and corresponding to the W173 feminist-narratology topic.
  • Group type: the seven Spider Sisters are a "kinship community" (living, bathing, and plotting together in Pansi Cave); the other seven are "lone demons", linked only loosely by karma, geography, or enmity.
  • Fate: of the 14 female demons, 10 are destroyed (the seven Spider Sisters + Scorpion Demon + White Bone Demon + Jade-Faced Fox + Apricot Fairy), and only 3 reach a returned/recruited/cultivated ending (Jade Rabbit Demon, Rat Demon, Princess Iron Fan) — a destruction rate of 71.4%.
  • Role of the collectors: Bodhisattva Pilanpo (whose son the Pleiades Star Lord killed the Scorpion Demon, and who subdued the Centipede Demon at Sun Wukong’s request), Bodhisattva Guanyin (who referred the Rat Demon to Li the Pagoda-Bearer), and Old Mother of Lishan (who once disguised herself as a mother and daughters to test the pilgrims) form the mirror image of "orthodox women".
  • Four dimensions: scoring on autonomy, combat, wisdom, and tragedy shows Princess Iron Fan highest overall (tragedy 75); the Seventh Spider Sister is the youngest yet the most tragic (70); the collectors score very high on wisdom but very low on tragedy (15–25).

II · Force-Directed Graph of the Female Demon Group

FORCE-DIRECTED GRAPH: 17 NODES × 32 EDGES
Definition: The force-directed graph takes 17 nodes (seven Spider Sisters + seven lone demons + three collectors) as vertices and 32 links as relations. Links fall into four types: sisterly bond (within the seven Spider Sisters), rival co-wives (Princess Iron Fan – Jade-Faced Fox), neighboring kin (geographically adjacent female demons), and recruitment (female deity → female demon). Node color marks group type (Spider Demon / Loner Demon / Collector), node size reflects degree, and link thickness reflects relationship strength.

III · Sankey Diagram of the Female Demons’ Fates

SANKEY: 14 MONSTERS × EXECUTORS × FATE TYPES
Definition: The Sankey diagram shows three layers of flow: 14 female demons → fate executors → outcome types. The executors fall into six kinds: Sun Wukong, Zhu Bajie, the Pleiades Star Lord (son of Pilanpo), the Taiyin Star Lord, Li the Pagoda-Bearer, and self-cultivation; the outcomes are grouped into four types: destroyed, returned, recruited, and cultivated. Flow thickness reflects the number of female demons. It reveals the narrative rule that destruction dominates the female demons’ fate, with returned/recruited/cultivated making up only 21.4%.

IV · Four-Dimension Radar Chart of the Female Demons

RADAR: AUTONOMY × COMBAT × WISDOM × TRAGEDY
