I · Twelve-Dimension Narratology Overview

OVERVIEW: 12-DIMENSION NARRATOLOGY FRAMEWORK
Definition:The twelve-dimension narratology network integrates the W161–W174 narratology topics, dividing the narratological study of Journey to the West into twelve interrelated dimensions: seven sensory dimensions (Time W161, Space W162, Auditory W163, Olfactory W164, Tactile W165, Visual W166, Gustatory W167) and five theoretical dimensions (Intertext W169, Rhetoric W170, Cognitive W171, Gender W173, Postcolonial W174). It builds on the W168 Six-Senses Narratology Network to realize the theoretical expansion from seven senses to twelve dimensions, constructing a complete narratological analysis network covering space-time, the senses, intertextuality, rhetoric, cognition, gender, and the postcolonial.

II · Twelve-Dimension Narratology Force-Directed Graph

FORCE-DIRECTED GRAPH: 12 DIMENSIONS & INTER-DIMENSION EDGES
Definition:The force-directed graph takes the twelve narratology dimensions as nodes; node color distinguishes five categories (spatiotemporal, sensory, theoretical, gender, postcolonial), and node size reflects academic value. Edges between dimensions are labeled by relation type (spatiotemporal, sensory, theoretical), with edge thickness corresponding to connection strength. It reveals the internal structure of twelve-dimension narratology — the seven sensory dimensions cluster tightly, the five theoretical dimensions connect laterally, and gender and the postcolonial form intersectional nodes.

III · Dimension → Theorist → Case Three-Layer Sankey

SANKEY: DIMENSIONS → THEORISTS → APPLICATIONS
Definition:The Sankey diagram shows a three-layer flow: twelve dimensions → related theorists → Journey to the West application cases. Each dimension connects to its representative theorists (e.g. Time links to Genette, Space to Bakhtin, Intertext to Kristeva), and theorists in turn flow into concrete Journey application cases (e.g. journey time structure, three-realm chronotope, intertextual network). Flow thickness corresponds to a theory’s application weight in Journey studies, revealing the transformation path from abstract theory to textual analysis.

IV · Twelve-Dimension Narratology Radar (4 metrics)

RADAR: 12 DIMENSIONS × 4 METRICS
Definition:The radar chart evaluates each narratology dimension on four metrics: theory depth (maturity of the theoretical system), text coverage (breadth of Journey to the West textual analysis), cross-topic links (degree of linkage with other dimensions), and academic value (research contribution and influence). The larger the radar area, the stronger the dimension’s overall standing within the twelve-dimension narratology framework. Intertextuality (Intertext), temporal narratology (Time), and cognitive narratology (Cognitive) show the strongest composite performance, while the tactile and olfactory dimensions are emerging growth points.
