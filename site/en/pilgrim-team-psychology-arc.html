⑥ Team Psychology Evolution Insights

[Trough] Chapter 27, Three Times Beating the White-Bone Demon, is the team’s absolute psychological trough. The five pilgrims’ mean cohesion falls to 4.6: Sun Wukong is banished, Zhu Bajie slanders, Tang Sanzang misjudges, Sha Wujing stays silent, and the White Dragon Horse has just joined and is not yet steady. This is the only chapter in the whole book of “master–disciple rupture,” and the most pronounced tension point of the “individual–team” two-layer.
[Repair] Chapter 31, the Yellow-Robed Monster incident, completes the team’s repair. Zhu Bajie travels to Flower-Fruit Mountain to goad Sun Wukong into returning, and Sun Wukong subdues the Yellow-Robed Monster to rescue his master. After this chapter the mean cohesion rebounds to 6.0, the “master–disciple mutual trust” mechanism is rebuilt, laying the foundation for the next 50 chapters of stability.
[Identity] Chapter 58, the True and False Monkey King, is Sun Wukong’s identity crisis. The composite psychological score drops to 5.0, matching ch27 as the two troughs of Sun Wukong’s curve. But this “crisis” is inward—not a master–disciple rupture but a crisis of Sun Wukong’s self-identity, ultimately resolved when Tathāgata exposes the Six-Eared Macaque and completes the “mind-monkey returns to oneness.”
[Sublimation] Chapters 81–100 are the team’s psychological sublimation period. After the final ordeal of the Bottomless Cave, the five pilgrims’ curves rise together, reaching 9–10 for all by ch100. This is the psychological completion of “becoming sages through surviving tribulations”—echoing the individual layer’s “buddhahood arc” in character-sentiment-arc.html.
[Divergence] The five pilgrims’ psychology curves show a “three-band divergence” pattern. Sun Wukong stays above the mean throughout (median 7); Tang Sanzang and the White Dragon Horse “open low and finish high” (rising from 0 to 10); Zhu Bajie stays low throughout (median 5). Sha Wujing is the most stable (IQR only 5–7), the team’s “psychological anchor.”
[Two-layer] This page and character-sentiment-arc.html form the “individual–team” two-layer. The individual layer focuses on single-character sentiment arcs (W194 narratology-13d), while the team layer (this page) focuses on five-pilgrim aggregation and cohesion evolution. Cross-examining the two layers shows that individual troughs do not necessarily correspond to team troughs, but the two “two-layer resonance points” at ch27/ch58 are key hubs of the book’s psychological narrative.
