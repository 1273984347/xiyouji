The Pilgrimage in Numbers

A few structural facts that frame the hundred-chapter journey.

Key structural insights

  • 81 tribulations across 14 years of travel, from Chang'an to Vulture Peak — a benchmark list of graded trials.
  • 53 named places across 26 regions, with caves (13) the most common site type — the demon lair as the novel's signature space.
  • 49 of 81 tribulations end with the demon "taken away" by a heavenly sponsor; only 25 end with the demon killed — backing decides fate.
  • 39 of 81 tribulations require Wukong to "call for backup" — the pilgrimage is a collaboration, not a solo performance.
  • Mount-related demons: 16 of 16 all taken away alive, 0 killed — the most striking "backing effect" in the whole novel.
