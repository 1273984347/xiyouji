① Three-Comparison Radar Chart · Mind-Nature / Governance / Remonstrance

Three comparison groups are scored (1-10) on three dimensions: "mind-nature theory / art of governance / remonstrance tradition". Each group has two polygons: solid fill for the Ming figure and dashed for the Journey to the West figure. Note the paired highs — Wang Yangming and Tang Sanzang on mind-nature, Zhang Juzheng and the Jade Emperor on governance, Hai Rui and Wukong on remonstrance.

② Nine-Dimension Comparison Matrix Heatmap · Theory / Practice / Attainment

A 9-cell matrix of 3 groups × 3 dimensions (Theory / Practice / Attainment), where color depth represents comparison strength (1-10). Four color tiers run from light to dark: light beige (1-3) / mid orange (4-6) / deep orange (7-8) / vermilion (9-10).

③ Ming Intellectual History Timeline · 1472-1592

The horizontal axis marks years, from Wang Yangming's birth (1472) to the printing of the Shidetang edition of Journey to the West (1592). Each node is a key event, colored by figure. Note Zhang Juzheng's rise after Wang Yangming's death in 1529, the deaths of Zhang Juzheng and Wu Cheng'en in the same year (1582), and the completion of Journey to the West in 1592.

[Dual track] The W232 document handles theoretical argumentation; the W233-1 visualization handles graphical presentation. Readers should read the W232 text and W233 charts together — the text answers "why this comparison", the visualization answers "how strong is the comparison".
[Intertextuality] The structural isomorphism between Wang Yangming's School of Mind and Tang Sanzang's mind-nature cultivation. Both take "mind" as substance, "practice" as effort, and "sagehood / buddhahood" as attainment. The paired highs (9/8) on the mind-nature dimension in the radar chart confirm this intertextuality.
[Tension] The echo and tension between Zhang Juzheng's statecraft and the Jade Emperor's governance. Both reach 9 on art of governance, but Zhang Juzheng's reform-versus-conservatism (6) falls short of the Jade Emperor's power structure (9) — the Jade Emperor is "statecraft already stabilized", Zhang Juzheng is "statecraft in progress".
[Resistance] The shared spiritual origin of Hai Rui's remonstrance and Wukong's resistance. Both reach 9 on remonstrance tradition / spirit of resistance. The difference lies in the "individual versus system" dimension: Hai Rui scores 8 (remonstrance to the death within the system), Wukong scores 7 (resistance outside the system) — same origin, different paths.
