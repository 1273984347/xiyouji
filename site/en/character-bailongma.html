Ao Lie, the White Dragon Horse

CHARACTER · DEEP DIVE
Third Prince of the West Sea, condemned for a burnt pearl, reborn as the beast that carries the monk — the pilgrim who speaks least and bears most.
A long-form English portrait of Ao Lie / Bai Long Ma (白龙马), grounded in the canonical narrative and the project's team/psychology datasets (pilgrim-team-dynamic-network, pilgrim-team-psychology-arc, character-relationship-3d). Belbin and radar figures are taken directly from those datasets; no numbers are invented.

Names & Identities ↔ prince to steed

He is Ao Lie (敖烈), Third Prince of the West Sea Dragon King (西海龙王三太子), better known as the White Dragon Horse (白龙马, also 小白龙). Condemned to death for accidentally burning a luminous pearl, he is spared by Guanyin and reborn as a slender white horse to carry the Tang monk across the wilderness. At the journey's end he is elevated to the Eightfold Heavenly Dragon Horse (八部天龙马). Of the five pilgrims he is the one whose human form we meet only briefly, and whose service is almost entirely silent.

The Beast Who Bears the Burden ↔ the unseen carrier

The project's relationship graph describes him tersely: "西海龙王三太子，化身为马驮唐僧。" — the third prince of the West Sea, transformed into a horse that carries Tang Sanzang. He is the only pilgrim whose entire visible function is to bear: not to fight, not to preach, not to complain, but to carry the saint and his scriptures on his back through a hundred trials. Redemption, for Ao Lie, is expressed as endurance made flesh.

Team Role ↔ Belbin: Specialist

His Belbin profile is the most specialised on the team: Specialist (8) and Implementer (5) lead, with a moderate Team Worker (4), while Coordinator, Shaper, and Plant all sit at 2. He is not a strategist or an idea-generator but the dedicated instrument that performs one task flawlessly — the mount that never stumbles, the role no one else could fill. The pilgrimage needs exactly one of him, and he is that one.

Psychology Profile ↔ radar: quiet courage

His five-axis radar reads Fear 3, Resolve 8, Anger 4, Wisdom 7, Obedience 8. The profile is striking: the lowest fear and the highest obedience and resolve on the team, paired with high wisdom. Ao Lie is the silent servant with a still, unshakable centre — courage not as noise but as the quiet willingness to carry what must be carried.

Why the Silent Pilgrim Matters ↔ the fifth mode

Where Wukong wins by force, Sanzang by faith, Bajie by surviving himself, and Sha by steadiness, Ao Lie wins by self-effacement. He is the book's proof that salvation includes those who labour without a name — the carrier, the beast of burden, the one whose heroism is to be useful and unseen. The five pilgrims are complete only with him.
