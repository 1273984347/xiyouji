Key Insights

What the structural skeleton of the hundred chapters looks like

  • 01The book closes with its heaviest cluster — "Ascension to Buddhahood" (Ch.78–97) holds 20 chapters, a fifth of the novel, while "Final Scroll, Truth Attained" (Ch.98–100) holds only 3; the three closing clusters (Ch.59–100) together carry 42 of the 100 chapters.
  • 02Longest and shortest chapters sit in opposite narrative phases — Ch.41 is the peak at 9,820 words inside "Heavenly Demons" (Ch.27–42), and Ch.9 is the floor at 4,150 words inside "Pilgrimage Begins" (Ch.8–12); the opening phase is the leanest stretch of the book.
  • 03The couplet matrix is a length-matching rule, not a style rule — its peak runs along the diagonal (upper and lower couplet of equal character count, score near 100) and falls about 12 points per step of length mismatch down to a floor near 15, so a chapter's couplet neatness is decided by whether the two lines match in length.
  • 0492 of the 100 chapter titles are couplet-regular — the 8 exceptions are the structural minority, and they fall where the narrative turns from set-piece episodes to the closing ascent.
