4 · Insights on the Difficulty Terrain

[Peak] Trial 74 — the Three Demons of Lion-Camel Ridge mark the book's peak difficulty (10)。The three demons — Azure Lion, White Elephant, and Golden-Winged Peng — are all mounts descended from the Buddha's court; Wukong is utterly defeated, and only Tathāgata himself, leading five hundred arhats, subdues Peng.This is the only ordeal requiring the Buddha's personal intervention, marking the pilgrimage's hardest test — the limit of Heaven's mobilization.
[Structure] "Descended mounts" monopolize the 9-10 extreme tier。Of the 8 extreme ordeals, 5 are descended mounts (Blue Ox of Gold-Bowl 9 / Even the Gods Cannot Subdue 9 / Three Demons of Lion-Camel 10 / Dark Clouds at the City 9 / Golden Gong in the Void 9)，Their shared trait: "high-ranking celestial mount + master reclaims it + taken-away ending." This confirms the "demon-backer theory" — a demon with connections is the hardest to beat.
[Curve] Difficulty follows a four-phase "low-rise-peak-fall" curve along the pilgrimage。Prequel's 4 ordeals average 2.5 (no combat)；Early (ch.13-23) average 3.6 (team-building, mostly recruiting disciples)；Mid (ch.24-58) average 6.8 (White Bone Demon thrice struck + false Monkey King + Flame Mountain — mind-demons and mounts interweave, difficulty climbs)；Late (ch.59-79) average 6.9 (Lion-Camel + Yuhua + Biqiu — mounts erupt in concentration, peak difficulty)；Final (ch.91-100) average 3.8 (return toward Buddhahood, back to low difficulty)。
[Contrast] "Mind-demons" split at the extremes。Among the 10 mind-demon ordeals, "White Bone Demon thrice struck / Wukong banished" (6) and "banished again" (7) are mid-level，but "the false macaque" (9 — Tathāgata unmasks the Six-Eared Macaque) reaches extreme。This reveals the unpredictability of "self-splitting" crises — a master-disciple quarrel is easily mended, but identity impersonation needs outside judgment.
[Coda] Ch.98-100's 14 ordeals drop off a cliff (average 3.4)。Lingyun Ford's rebirth, Scriptures Incomplete, Thunderclap at Buddha's Realm, Five Sages Attain the Truth — all "procedural passings" with no demon opposition.This echoes the book's "cultivating the mind" theme — once at Spirit Mountain, difficulty yields to the ritualized narrative of "perfecting the heart."
