Zhu Bajie, the Pig

CHARACTER · DEEP DIVE
Field-Marshal Tianpeng fallen to swine, Altar-Cleaner of leftovers — appetite, comedy, and the most human of the pilgrims.
A long-form English portrait of Zhu Bajie, grounded in the canonical narrative and the project's team/psychology datasets (pilgrim-team-dynamic-network, pilgrim-team-psychology-arc, character-relationship-3d). Belbin and radar figures are taken directly from those datasets.

Names & Identities ↔ marshal to pig

He is Zhu Bajie (猪八戒, "Pig of the Eight Precepts"), given the Dharma name Zhu Wuneng (猪悟能, "Awakened to Ability"). Before his fall he was Field-Marshal Tianpeng (天蓬元帅), a commander of the heavenly navy, demoted to a pig-demon for drunkenly harassing the Moon's Lady Chang'e. At the journey's end he is rewarded as the Altar-Cleaner (净坛使者), patron of leftovers. The slide from marshal to swine to half-comic glutton is the joke the whole book runs on — and also its most honest portrait of appetite.

The Earthy One ↔ honest about wanting

The project's network describes him tersely: "天蓬元帅转世，贪吃好色但忠心耿耿。". Where Wukong is the restless mind and Sanzang the stubborn will, Bajie is the body — hungry, lustful, lazy, homesick, and therefore the most recognisably human of the three. His constant wish to split the luggage and go home is the reader's own fatigue given a voice.

Team Role ↔ Belbin: social glue

In Belbin terms Bajie scores highest on Resource Investigator (8) and Teamworker (8) — the roles that keep a group sociable and unblocked — but lowest on Completer-Finisher (2). He is the one who defuses tension with a joke and the one who abandons the post when things get hard. The team needs his warmth; it can never trust his discipline.

Psychology Profile ↔ radar: low fear, low wrath

His five-axis radar reads Fear 8, Resolve 4, Anger 6, Wisdom 4, Obedience 5. He is not especially afraid and not especially angry; what he lacks is the discipline axis. Compared with Sanzang's obedience (9) and Wukong's resolve (9), Bajie's middle values are exactly his charm and his liability — a creature of the moment.

Comic Relief, and More ↔ the body's truth

Bajie is the book's comedy, but the comedy is structural. A pilgrimage that only contained a flawless sage and a flawless saint would be a sermon; Bajie is the appetite that must be carried the whole way and never quite tamed. His failures — sleeping through guard duty, believing the easiest story, wanting the pretty girl — are the reader's failures, forgiven in advance.
