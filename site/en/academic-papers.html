SCHOLARLY BIBLIOGRAPHY — ENGLISH SELECTION
Ten representative entries from the project's 55-entry scholarly bibliography.

The project maintains a 55-entry scholarly bibliography covering original editions, classical commentary, modern monographs, overseas translations, ideological sources, and recent research. The table below selects ten representative entries with English titles or English-translatable significance, drawn from all eight categories of the full index.

The complete 55-entry bibliography (in Chinese, with GB/T 7714 citations) is in Academic Paper Index (Chinese).
Ten representative academic papers from the Journey to the West bibliography
ID Author Year Title Journal / Publisher Topic
V01 Shidetang 1592 Newly Printed Official Large-Character Journey to the West with Images Jinling Shidetang woodblock edition Earliest surviving complete 100-chapter edition; the project's base text.
A01 Lu Xun 1923 A Brief History of Chinese Fiction People's Literature Publishing House Classified the novel as "shenmo xiaoshuo" (god-and-demon fiction); disenchanted the alchemical reading.
A02 Hu Shi 1923 Research on the Authorship of Journey to the West In: Hu Shi Wen Cun, Vol. 2 Textual research on authorship; doubted Wu Cheng'en; warned against over-reading.
A03 Chen Yinque 1930 On the Evolution of Xuanzang's Disciples' Stories in Journey to the West Bulletin of the Institute of History and Philology, Academia Sinica, 2(2) Traced Wukong's lineage to Hanuman in the Ramayana via translated Buddhist sutras.
A04 Zheng Zhenduo 1933 The Evolution of Journey to the West Shibao · Appreciation Weekly Built a stemma of textual evolution from folk legend to the 100-chapter novel.
T01 Anthony C. Yu 1977–1983 The Journey to the West (4 vols., English translation) University of Chicago Press Landmark scholarly English translation preserving religious complexity.
T03 Arthur Waley 1942 Monkey (English abridged translation) George Allen & Unwin, London Literary abridged translation; shaped Anglophone reception of the novel.
T05 Andrew H. Plaks 1987 The Four Masterworks of the Ming Novel: Ssu ta ch'i-shu Princeton University Press Structuralist reading placing the novel among the "Four Masterworks"; irony as rhetoric.
T08 Eva Lüdi Kong 2016 Die Reise in den Westen (German complete translation) Reclam, Stuttgart First complete German translation; 2017 Leipzig Book Fair translation prize.
N02 Zhu Hongbo & Zhang Peiheng 2021 Digital Humanities Research on Journey to the West: Chapter-Title Word Frequencies of the 100-Chapter Edition Wenxue Yichan (Literary Heritage), 2021(3) Quantitative study of chapter-title characters; a digital-humanities paradigm for the novel.
