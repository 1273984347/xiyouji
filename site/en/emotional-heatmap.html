Heatmap Matrix

The horizontal axis is the 20 selected chapters (in chapter order); the vertical axis is the 8 critics. Each cell's color maps to sentiment polarity (positive = green / neutral = yellow / negative = red), while its opacity maps to reaction intensity (1–10, the deeper the stronger). Hover a cell to see its quote tooltip; click a cell or a scholar card below to lock/unlock the row highlight.

Sentiment Curves

One polyline per figure; the horizontal axis is the 20 ordeal numbers, the vertical axis is reaction intensity (1–10). Each segment's color maps to that segment's sentiment polarity, and the endpoint dots use the same color mapping. The line-end label is the scholar's name. Hover a dot to see details.

Scholar Cards

8 cards, each with name · dates · school · representative saying, plus the positive / neutral / negative counts and average intensity across the 20 ordeals. Hover highlights the corresponding heatmap row; click locks the highlight (keyboard Enter / Space also supported).
